# YouCook Delivers 🍽️🚚

**YouCook Delivers** is an innovative food delivery platform that bridges the gap between YouTube cooking content and real-world food delivery. It enables users to order meals directly from food content creators or home chefs whose dishes they've seen on video. By combining the power of video content, local chefs, and delivery logistics, YouCook Delivers brings your screen's food fantasies to your doorstep.

## 🌟 Features

- 🔍 **Discover & Watch**: Browse YouTube cooking videos and discover delicious dishes from content creators.
- 🛒 **Order What You See**: Place an order for meals featured in the video directly through the website.
- 🧑‍🍳 **creator Dashboard**: Allows content creators and home chefs to manage orders and menus.
- 🚗 **Delivery Management**: Integrated delivery system for tracking, accepting, and routing orders.
- ⭐ **Rate & Review**: Customers can review meals after receiving and enjoying them.
- 📍 **Google Maps Integration**: Real-time route display for delivery personnel.

## 🖼️ UI Highlights
